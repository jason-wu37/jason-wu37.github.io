I am an AI Researcher at Kaon Labs, where I work on AI personalization, user modeling, and LLM post-training.

My research aims to improve the efficiency and trustworthiness of AI systems through a fundamental understanding of how they learn and behave.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Lav Varshney. My research interests lie broadly in large language models and agents, safety, security, interpretability, AI for Science, continual learning, and multimodal learning.
